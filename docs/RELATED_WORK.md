# Related Work

This repository starts from classical objects rather than claiming new versions
of them.

- The distinct prime-divisor function `omega(n)`, which appears as the
  twelve-o'clock anchor `D(n, 0)`.
- The Erdos-Kac theorem for the normal order and distribution of `omega(n)`.
  PrimeClock Dynamics treats this as motivation for an angular field question,
  not as a proved extension.
- Additive arithmetic functions and residue-class additive functions. The
  fixed-angle selector rewrites `D_x(n, alpha)` as a finite prime-indexed sum of
  residue-class indicators.
- Bernoulli sums and triangular-array central limit methods. These are natural
  tools for fixed-cutoff CRT moment and possible CLT preparation, while the
  diagonal model remains separate.
- Mertens' product theorem for `prod_{p <= n}(1 - 1/p)` and its sieve-density
  interpretation. This artifact separates the fixed-cutoff CRT average
  identity from the later diagonal Mertens-scale problem.
- The Chinese remainder theorem as finite independence across prime residue
  coordinates over a complete CRT period.
- Residue-class discrepancy and the Finite-Window Periodic Discrepancy Bound,
  which are the first bridge from complete CRT periods to finite windows.
- Random interval covering models on the circle, including Dvoretzky-type
  covering questions. These are comparison tools rather than current claims.
- Prime Reciprocal Covering as a separate finite artifact program.

The first research task is to define a clean bridge from the Prime Clock visual
model to these existing topics without overstating the result level.
