# Shrinking-Angle Gaussian Field Related Work

Status: v1.1.0 public artifact related-work note.

The v1.1 theorem is closest to the Kubilius probabilistic model for additive
arithmetic functions: complete-CRT independence makes the prime-indexed residue
coordinates independent, and the proof uses a bounded triangular-array
Cramer-Wold argument.

The fixed-angle `alpha=0` fiber recovers the prime-divisor counting coordinate
from the earlier PrimeClock Dynamics work. The v1.1 theorem moves from fixed
angles to moving-angle arrays in the complete-CRT model.

The phrase `Shrinking-Angle Gaussian Field` is used cautiously. The main theorem
is kernel-conditioned, and the zero-ray corollary gives one concrete
shrinking-angle family. General distance-only kernel laws are not claimed.

This is not standard Gaussian Multiplicative Chaos. Standard GMC starts from an
ordinary logarithmically correlated Gaussian field, often with a `-log |x-y|`
singularity. The PrimeClock collision structure is prime-indexed and has a
log-log scale in the expected shrinking regimes. Finite-beta Angular Kubilius
Chaos remains a later program.
